✨ Your Gateway to the eCash Drivechain Universe ✨

Sidecoin puts a whole ecosystem of sidechains in your pocket. One wallet, one seed, and a growing community of networks built on Bitcoin's security.

⚡ WHAT YOU CAN DO
• 🛡️ Track and manage 10 live sidechains, with more on the way
• 💸 Send and receive with on-device signing and a clear review step
• 📷 Scan any QR code to pay in seconds
• 🌍 Explore the Drivechain web with the built-in browser
• 🔐 Unlock with biometrics — just one prompt per session

🚧 DEPOSIT & WITHDRAW — IN DEVELOPMENT
Moving value between L1 and a sidechain is shown in the app for review, but the deposit and withdrawal flows are not live yet — the buttons are clearly marked as under development.

🌐 THE SIDECHAINS
• ⚡ Thunder Network — fast, low-fee everyday payments
• 🌨️ Snowside — full EVM smart contracts, secured by Bitcoin
• 🕵️ zSide — private, shielded transactions
• 🏷️ BitNames — human-readable names and identity
• 💎 BitAssets — tokens, NFTs, and digital assets
• ⚛️ Photon — quantum-resistant cryptography
• 🎯 Truthcoin — decentralized prediction markets
• 🔄 CoinShift — trustless cross-chain swaps
• 🧬 RISCy — RISC-V innovation
• 🏦 FreeBank — Bitcoin-native banking
• ➕ Elements Plus — announced, coming soon (no BIP-300 slot assigned yet)

🧪 TEST NETWORKS
Before the eCash fork activates, Sidecoin runs against the public signet and betanet test networks — the same app, the same wallet, free test coins. L1 reads and broadcasts go to community Esplora endpoints (esplora.signet.drivechain.info, esplora.beta.ecash.ninja); the market price card reads eCash Farm.

🪶 LIGHTWEIGHT BY DESIGN
• No full chain download — talks to public Esplora nodes
• No accounts, no sign-up, no custodian

🌱 Powered by BIP-300 Hashrate Escrows and BIP-301 Blind Merged Mining — trustless sidechains secured by Bitcoin's full SHA-256d hashrate.
